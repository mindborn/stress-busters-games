🧩 Stress Busters

Little offline-friendly image puzzle games — pick a picture, unwind, rebuild it.

🔲Swap Puzzle

Your picture sliced into rectangular tiles and shuffled. Tap or drag to swap tiles back into place — correct tiles bloom into colour and lock in. Custom grids, crop tool, auto-solve.

▶ Play 🖼️Jigsaw Fusion

Classic jigsaw feel — pieces snap home and fuse seamlessly with their placed neighbours until the photo becomes whole again.

▶ Play 🤖Android app

Swap Puzzle as an installable Android app (signed release APK, ~7 MB). Also available: debug build.
